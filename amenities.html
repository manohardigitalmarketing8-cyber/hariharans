Every photographer, cinematographer, editor and event captain on our roster is trained in-house — so the quality you see in our portfolio is the quality you get, every single time.

Accessibility Promise

Wheelchair Seating & Accessible Venue Setup

Celebrations are for everyone — including grandparents, elders and guests with mobility needs. Every venue plan we execute includes dedicated accessible arrangements, planned at the layout stage rather than added as an afterthought.

  • Ramped, step-free entry paths at every gate
  • Reserved wheelchair spaces with clear stage view
  • Extra-wide aisles kept clear throughout the event
  • Accessible restrooms located near the seating block
  • Priority parking bays closest to the entrance
  • Trained ushers assisting elders all day long

Just tell us your guests' needs while booking — accessibility arrangements are included at no extra cost.
